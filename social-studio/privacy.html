Social Studio

Privacy policy

Social Studio is operated by Yaghmood. For privacy questions or requests, contact yaghmood@gmail.com.

What the app does

Social Studio is a personal app for its owner to manage their own social media accounts. It runs on the owner's computer and helps plan content, store reference materials and connect supported accounts. The app sends requests to a platform when an account is connected or a feature needs that platform.

Information processed

Information you enter can include account details, content plans, notes, images and video links. The app stores some information in your browser and some in files on your computer. If you download a video reference, the video and its metadata are stored locally.

When you connect a social account, the app requests permission to access the information needed for its features. TikTok provides basic profile information, including the display name and account identifier. Instagram and YouTube connections can provide account information, media and available statistics. The app stores connection credentials and account details on the computer. YouTube also permits editing channel description and keywords when that feature is used.

When the owner chooses to post a video from the app, the app uploads that video, its title and caption, and the choices made on the post screen (such as who can watch it) to the connected account on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat or Dailymotion. Nothing is posted without the owner's confirmation. The app does not collect or post on behalf of anyone else.

Where information is sent

TikTok, Meta, Google, Snap and Dailymotion receive authorization and API requests when you use their integrations. Video services receive requests when you load previews or download references. Google Fonts may receive requests when the app loads its typefaces. Those services process information under their own policies:

  • TikTok privacy policy
  • Meta privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy
  • Snap privacy policy
  • Dailymotion privacy policy

This website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about visits under its privacy statement.

Storage and deletion

Connected account credentials are protected using Windows Data Protection for the current Windows account. Other local files and browser storage depend on the security of your device. Local data stays on your computer until you delete it, clear the relevant browser storage or remove the app's data files. Backups may contain copies.

You can disconnect an account in Social Studio to remove its saved connection. Disconnecting does not necessarily revoke access at the provider or remove separate local data. Review authorized applications in the relevant platform's settings to revoke access there. Instagram may retain a separate saved Facebook login in the app.

Your choices and rights

You may request information about personal data and exercise any rights that apply to you by contacting yaghmood@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, these may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. You may also contact your data protection authority. The app is local, so we may need your help identifying data stored only on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page.
